# Python for finance

Notebooks for the course **Data Analytics and Financial Modeling**, MSc Finance & Investment Banking, Grenoble Ecole de Management.

## How to use a notebook

1. Click the **Open in Colab** link of the notebook (on Moodle or in the table below).
2. Sign in with your Google account. Nothing to install.
3. To keep your work: **File > Save a copy in Drive**. You work on your copy; this repo does not change.

Data files are in `data/`. The notebooks load them directly from this repo.
